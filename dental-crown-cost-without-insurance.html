Dental crown cost without insurance: materials, extra fees and quote checklist

A crown quote can mean just the restoration—or the total work needed to prepare and protect the tooth. Learn what to compare before agreeing to treatment.

Published November 10, 2026 · By DentaOferta editorial team · General U.S. patient information

Quick answer

Published U.S. procedure-cost research lists average reference fees of $1,399 for a porcelain crown, $1,211 for a metallic crown and $1,114 for a porcelain-fused-to-metal crown. These are not DentaOferta clinic offers or guaranteed prices; your exam, build-up, imaging and other work may be quoted separately.

What does a dental crown quote include?

A dental crown fits over a prepared natural tooth. Treatment may include a dental exam, imaging as clinically needed, preparation, impressions or digital scanning, a temporary crown, a laboratory-made or same-day crown, and adjustment. A quote can omit some of those items.

  • Initial assessment: ask whether examination and any required X-rays are billed separately.
  • Tooth preparation: confirm whether a filling, core build-up or post is needed before the crown.
  • Temporary crown: check whether an interim crown and its replacement are included.
  • Final crown: ask about the proposed material, laboratory work, placement and bite adjustments.
  • Extra visits: ask who pays if the crown needs remaking or adjustments after placement.

Natural tooth crown versus implant crown

This guide covers a crown placed on an existing tooth. A crown on a dental implant is a different restoration and may require an implant fixture, abutment or special components. Do not assume the above averages are the price of an implant crown package.

If your tooth is missing or cannot be restored, read our single-tooth implant cost guide. If you already have an implant treatment plan, use the itemized implant quote calculator rather than mixing two unrelated totals.

Two example estimates: why the lower crown price may not win

Illustration only, not current dental prices: Clinic A lists a $900 crown but also $180 for an exam, $250 for a core build-up and $120 for a temporary, for a total of $1,450. Clinic B quotes $1,300 for a crown package including those services. In this hypothetical comparison, Clinic B’s larger headline fee is the smaller total. Confirm equivalent treatment plans and clinical quality first.

Make a separate line for each service. Never compare a natural-tooth crown with an implant-and-crown package as if they were the same procedure.

Insurance, savings and payment questions

For people without dental insurance, request a written cash-pay or self-pay estimate. Some offices offer phased payments, membership programs or third-party financing. Ask for the total repayment cost, interest and fees before signing a financing agreement.

For insured patients, crowns may be subject to deductibles, waiting periods, yearly limits or restrictions. Adult dental coverage is not a mandatory essential health benefit in U.S. Marketplace medical plans, so you should check the actual dental policy rather than assume coverage. HealthCare.gov explains adult dental plan options.

Crown estimate checklist to bring to the dentist

  • What diagnosis requires a crown, and are less extensive restorations appropriate?
  • Which material is recommended and why?
  • Does the total include exam, imaging, preparation, temporary crown and fitting?
  • Could root canal treatment or build-up be required? What would those services cost?
  • What are the remake, aftercare and adjustment policies?
  • Can I receive a written line-item estimate before treatment begins?

Ask a licensed dentist to examine the tooth before you decide. Photos or pricing articles cannot establish whether a crown is appropriate.

Frequently asked questions

What is the average cost of a crown without insurance?

Published U.S. procedure-cost research lists material-specific reference averages, including $1,399 for porcelain and $1,114 for porcelain-fused-to-metal crowns. Your actual local, all-in bill may differ substantially.

Is a temporary crown the same as a permanent crown?

No. Temporary restorations serve a different role and should not be price-compared with a final crown as an equivalent long-term service.

Does a dental crown price include a root canal?

Usually treat root canal therapy and the crown as separate line items unless a provider explicitly includes both in the written plan.

Can a clinic quote a crown from a picture alone?

A photograph may help explain a concern, but diagnosis and final treatment planning require a professional evaluation.
