What the calculator includes and excludes

The tool multiplies three per-implant amounts (implant surgery, abutment and crown) by the implant count, then adds case-level charges you enter. It subtracts only an insurance payment or adjustment that you manually provide, and never lets the result fall below zero. Blank fields are treated as zero for arithmetic only, not as proof a service is free or unnecessary.

Formula: (implant count × [placement + abutment + crown]) + exam + imaging + extraction + graft + temporary restoration + sedation + other fees − entered insurance adjustment.

It does not determine which procedures are necessary, predict whether an implant can be placed, estimate local prices from your ZIP code or verify insurance benefits. For pricing context, use our published implant cost guide and FAIR Health Consumer ZIP-code dental cost lookup.

How to compare two clinic quotes accurately

  1. Ask both clinics for itemized written treatment plans based on your dental evaluation.
  2. Check that both proposals cover the same number of teeth and comparable services.
  3. Enter the amounts from Clinic A, then copy or print the breakdown.
  4. Clear the form and enter Clinic B's amounts; compare written totals and missing line items.
  5. Ask about potential extra work, aftercare, guarantee terms, safety, clinician qualifications and financing costs—not only price.

Some estimates may change after examination or imaging. If plans recommend different clinical approaches, ask a dentist to explain the difference before using the totals as a price comparison.

A simple worked example

Suppose a hypothetical quote includes $1,700 for placement, $450 for an abutment and $1,200 for a crown. For one implant that is $3,350. Add an illustrative $180 consultation and $240 imaging fee, and the arithmetic total is $3,770. These numbers are deliberately just a demonstration of the calculator, not sourced market prices and not a clinic quote.

If the office’s implant package already includes the abutment and crown, entering both the package and those component fees would overstate the total. Ask the clinic which charges are included.

Checklist: questions to ask before signing a quote

  • Is the implant post, abutment and final crown included?
  • Are exam and imaging fees billed separately?
  • Is bone grafting or extraction anticipated?
  • Are sedation, temporary restorations and follow-up appointments included?
  • Are there possible additional fees if my care plan changes?
  • Do these amounts reflect current written rates, insurance rules and financing terms?

Frequently asked questions

Does this calculator show the average U.S. dental implant price?

No. This is an itemized arithmetic worksheet. It only calculates fees entered by the user. The demonstration inputs are hypothetical and are not estimates of current prices.

Does it include the crown and abutment?

There are separate fields for them so you can enter your written quote. If your office includes them in a package, do not enter those fees a second time.

Can it calculate full-mouth implant costs?

It can multiply per-implant line items, but it is not designed to quote full-arch bridges or complex full-mouth reconstruction. Those require their own itemized plan.

Are my figures sent to DentaOferta?

No. The calculator runs in your browser. Entered amounts are not submitted to a server by this form. Printing or copying happens only when you choose those actions.
